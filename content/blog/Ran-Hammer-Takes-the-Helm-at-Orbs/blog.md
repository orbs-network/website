Orbs has appointed Ran Hammer as Chief Executive Officer, with Nadav Shemesh moving into the role of Executive Chairman.

Ran has been part of Orbs since the project began in 2017. His appointment brings someone who has helped shape the business, knows its partners, and understands its community into the CEO role.

As Chief Business Officer, Ran led the integration efforts across the full Orbs protocol suite, working with the team and partners to bring its products into use across the DeFi ecosystem. His work turned individual integrations into lasting relationships and built the partner network that supports Orbs today. Alongside that, he has been a familiar voice for the project, sharing product developments and engaging with the community on its future.

That work has given Ran a broad understanding of the business, from the technology and the teams building it to the partners putting it to use. His appointment builds on that established leadership, bringing years of hands-on experience across the project into the CEO role.

## Building on the work of the team

Orbs has evolved considerably since 2017. Its work with decentralized exchanges has brought advanced trading tools and better on-chain execution, showing how its infrastructure can support more sophisticated financial applications.

That progress reflects the work of the Orbs team, its partners, and the community supporting the network. Ran has been closely involved in that development and will now lead the business into its next stage.

Nadav remains part of that future. As Executive Chairman, he will focus on long-term strategy and support the leadership team. The transition builds on his contribution as CEO while giving Ran responsibility for leading the company forward.

## Looking ahead

Ran takes on the CEO role as Orbs continues to develop its technology and expand its ecosystem. His experience across the project's business, partnerships, and community will help guide that next stage.

For Ran, the role is new. The project, the relationships, and the work ahead are familiar. His appointment puts that experience at the center of Orbs' next stage.

## About Orbs

Orbs is a decentralized Layer-3 blockchain that enables more sophisticated applications on-chain. Powered by Proof-of-Stake, it provides an additional execution layer that enables more complex functionality beyond the native capabilities of smart contracts. Existing Orbs-powered protocols support advanced trading orders, liquidity aggregation and decentralized derivatives.
